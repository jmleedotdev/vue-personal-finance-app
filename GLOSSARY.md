# Personal Finance

Vocabulary for the personal finance app.

## Language

**Budget**:
A monthly spending limit for one transaction category. A category can have at most one budget; removing a budget does not remove its transactions.

**Maximum Spend**:
The positive amount allowed by a budget for a reporting month.

**Spent**:
The total outgoing transaction amount in a budget's category during the reporting month, including spending above its Maximum Spend.

**Remaining**:
The unspent portion of a budget's Maximum Spend, or zero when spending meets or exceeds that limit.

**Latest Spending**:
The most recent outgoing transactions in a budget's category, regardless of reporting month.

**Pot**:
A named savings goal with a target amount and an amount currently saved.

**Total Saved**:
The sum of the amounts currently saved across all pots, including pots outside the visible portion of a list.

**Recurring Bill**:
A monthly outgoing payment obligation identified by its payee. Its latest recorded payment establishes the amount and billing day; recurring bill payments cannot have positive amounts.

**Reporting Date**:
The calendar date as of which payment history and the reporting month's bill summaries are evaluated.

**Billing Day**:
The day of the month established by a recurring bill's latest recorded payment. A shorter month uses its last day without changing the established billing day.

**Total Bills**:
The combined monthly amount of all distinct recurring bills for the reporting month, including both paid and unpaid bills.

**Paid Bills**:
The total amount for recurring bills with a recorded payment in the reporting month on or before the reporting date, counting each bill once.

**Total Upcoming**:
The total amount for recurring bills still unpaid in the reporting month, including overdue bills but excluding future months.

**Due Soon**:
The portion of Total Upcoming due on or before seven calendar days after the reporting date, including overdue bills and bills due today.
